# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behave professionally. Expected: kind communication, respecting differing viewpoints, graceful acceptance of constructive criticism, focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable: harassment of any kind, trolling, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without consent, sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the repository maintainers (private security advisory or direct contact). Maintainers will review and respond proportionately, up to and including ban from the project spaces.
